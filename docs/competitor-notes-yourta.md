# Competitor notes — YOURTA (the-yourta.webflow.io)

Reviewed 2026-10-16. YOURTA: London-based (14 Cavendish Square), founder Dameli Kaliyeva (Kazakh heritage), yurts made in Kyrgyzstan on Lake Issyk-Kul, design by Dunie Design (Almaty). Lifestyle/aspirational brand. They PUBLISH prices (EUR).

No verbatim scraping remains in our live copy. None of their distinctive terms appear in ours. One soft echo left for Yossi's call: our story quote "rests lightly on the earth" vs their "travel lightly on the earth" (common phrase).

## Worth considering — integrate in OUR way, not copied
1. **Event / gathering capacity per size.** They give a standing/gathering figure per size (Standard 28m² ~30 people, Large 51m² ~65, XL 113m² ~150). We only give sleeping numbers. A "how many it gathers" figure would help resorts planning event and dining spaces. Add in our restrained idiom, as guidance, not a boast.
2. **A dedicated Interiors showcase.** They have a standalone "Interiors" page selling the finished guest experience. Ours is spread across Gallery + the trade "finished suite". Consider one clear, visual "what the guest walks into" interiors section/page.
3. **A few named, restrained finish/palette directions.** They productise interiors into two sets (Serenity / Joy) and three palettes (Mistral / Balearic / Tian Shan) so a buyer can choose fast. We deliberately killed the ethnic-decor upsell menu, so DO NOT reintroduce that. But a small set of named ARCHITECTURAL directions (e.g. tonal/material moods, undyed vs warm vs stone) in our clean idiom could help hospitality buyers choose without a cart. Handle carefully.
4. **Events / celebrations positioning.** They frame yurts as celebration venues (weddings, gatherings for 150). We touch this only on the 11-12m sizes. Lean into the gatherings angle where it is true (grand halls, arrival pavilions, dining), for resort buyers.
5. **Warranty.** They offer a 2-year warranty as standard; buyers may expect one. We offer none until we have grounds. Park with the certifications research (docs/certifications-future) and revisit together.

## What NOT to take
Published prices (we quote on a call), the lifestyle "way of life" voice (ours is grounded), named outside designers (our makers are the story), their "no planning permission needed" simplification (we defer honestly to the buyer's authority).

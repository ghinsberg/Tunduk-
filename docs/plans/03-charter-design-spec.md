# Tündük — "The Charter" Design Spec
_Approved direction. June 2026. Build the authority spine first: homepage, For Hotels & Resorts, Heritage/Charter._

## Positioning
Tündük is the authority on the boz üy, the keeper of the charter, the supplier that holds the standard of the Kyrgyz tent. The design must say "we hold the category" the instant a buyer lands, B2B-first, volume-ready, heritage-anchored.

## Design language (Direction B — The Charter)
**Palette**
- Parchment `#E8E0D1` (base) · warm white `#FAF7F0`
- Ink `#1A1410` (text + dark sections) · ink-mid `#4A3728` · ink-soft `#8A7060`
- **Oxblood `#6E2A24`** — the authority accent (labels, rules, seal). Replaces the old brighter terracotta.
- Gold `#C4923A` — crown + seal detail only, sparingly.

**Type**
- Playfair Display — headlines (700/800 command; italic for grace)
- Archivo — eyebrows, labels, buttons, proof-numbers (uppercase, tracked)
- Cormorant Garamond — long-form body (warmth)

**Motif & components**
- Tündük crown (roof-wheel, the mark on the Kyrgyz flag) as recurring device: dividers, watermark, seal centre.
- Wax-seal emblem: "Keepers of the Boz Üy · Kyzyl-Tuu · UNESCO."
- Big numbered proof-stats; archival "record" blocks; manifesto on ink; specimen-style captions.
- Subtle paper grain on parchment; full-ink sections for rhythm.

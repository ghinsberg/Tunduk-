# Reference materials — content research for Tündük

Source material for writing **original** Tündük blog/journal posts and page copy.
This folder lives under `docs/` which is **held out of the live deploy**, so nothing here
is published. These are third-party works — use them for **facts and inspiration only**,
never copy the prose. Facts aren't copyrightable; wording is.

> Tündük voice reminder when we write from these: warm, direct, specific; "Boz Üy"
> capitalised; British spelling; no em dashes; no "glamping" in the narrative voice
> (SEO plumbing only); sell the architecture, not ethnicity; **earn every claim**.

## 1. `yurt-architecture_giuroiu-2024.md`
- **What:** Long, thorough general article — "Yurt: Architecture, History, Sustainability, Materials and Typical Prices."
- **Source / author:** Anton Giuroiu, published 7 June 2024. **Copyrighted third-party article.** Do not reproduce verbatim or lightly reworded. Mine it for facts and structure ideas only.
- **Useful for us:** broad coverage of yurt history, architectural features (lattice/khana, compression ring, felt), sustainability, off-grid living, the room-by-room living angle, and the informational questions people ask — which map straight onto our GEO keyword targets ("what is a yurt", "yurt vs ger", "how long does a yurt last", "what is a yurt made of").
- **⚠ CAUTION — claims in this article that CONFLICT with Tündük and must NOT be carried over:**
  - **Prices** ($7k–$50k+). Tündük publishes **no prices** ("quoted on a call"). Never put these numbers on our site.
  - **"Fire-retardant" / fire-rated fabrics.** Tündük makes **no fire claims** (our felt is natural wool; fire safety defers to the buyer's local authority). Do not repeat.
  - **"Structural engineering standards for wind/snow/seismic loads", "commercial-grade hardware", "weather-resistant steel", "certified".** These describe modern Western (e.g. Pacific Yurts) builds, not our Boz Üy. We supply a data pack; certification comes from the client's own engineer. Don't claim it.
  - **Wood stove / central smoke hole for guest use.** The article assumes a central wood-burning stove. Tündük recommends **air conditioning** for climate control and does not sell the stove. (The tündük crown's smoke-hole role is heritage context, not a guest feature.)
  - **Heavy ger/"Mongolian yurt" conflation and "no single inventor".** Our whole position is the **Kyrgyz Boz Üy is distinct from the ger** (curved uuk, no central posts). Use this article's ger facts only to draw the contrast, never to blur it.
  - Generic "canvas/architectural fabric" covers — ours is **handmade sheep-wool felt**; don't describe our cover as canvas.
  - "Yurts aren't popular with homebuyers / niche counter-cultural" framing — irrelevant and off-brand for a B2B hospitality supplier. Ignore.

## 2. `bedouin-tent-design_excerpt.md`
- **What:** Short, lyrical passage on the black goat-hair Bedouin tent as climate-responsive design (the "breathing membrane", the weave that swells shut in rain, the tent that composts back to the soil).
- **Source / author:** Reads like **William McDonough & Michael Braungart, *Cradle to Cradle*** (the "desert skyscraper" / breathing-membrane passage). **Treat as a copyrighted quotation** — verify the exact source before using even a short quote, and if quoted, attribute it. Do not present as our own words.
- **Useful for us:** aligns beautifully with the **story page's existing Bedouin opener** ("Forty-seven years, a tent dweller" / "It started with a Bedouin tent"). Good conceptual fuel for a piece on tents as intelligent, climate-adapted architecture — the through-line from the Bedouin tent to the Boz Üy. Write our own prose; at most one short attributed quote.

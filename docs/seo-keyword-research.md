## Strategic reads

1. **The B2B exact-match terms barely get searched.** "yurt supplier" and "commercial glamping" return no data; "yurt manufacturer" and "hotel tent" are tiny. Hotels don't find suppliers by typing "yurt supplier" into Google — the B2B journey runs through referrals, trade, and AI answers. So our SEO win is **not** chasing thin B2B terms. It's: (a) ranking for the mid-volume *product* terms a developer actually types, and (b) winning the AI/GEO layer.

2. **"glamping yurt" is the single best target.** 1K–10K/mo, and it's literally what we sell. Primary keyword for the product/configure pages.

3. **The authentic lane is low-volume but uncontested.** `kyrgyz yurt`, `authentic yurt`, `traditional yurt`, `wooden yurt` — small traffic, but Low/Medium competition and *perfect* intent. Anyone searching these is our buyer and nobody's optimising for them. Own them completely — cheap, honest, high-conversion.

4. **"mongolian yurt" (1K–10K, Low competition) is a free lunch.** People conflate Mongolian and Kyrgyz yurts. One honest "Mongolian vs Kyrgyz yurt / ger vs boz üy" passage on the heritage page catches this traffic truthfully.

5. **"glamping" itself (100K–1M, Low competition) is tempting but a trap for us.** Massive volume, but broad/informational, low buying intent, off-brand for the voice, and dominated by directories. Don't build for the head term; harvest its long tail (`glamping tent`, `glamping yurt`, `glamping pods`, `glamping accommodation`) instead.

6. **Head term "yurt" (100K–1M, High) we can't win on page 1** against Pacific Yurts et al. We ride it only through long-tail + GEO, not head-on.

## Recommended keyword → page mapping (draft)

- **Home / title:** authentic Kyrgyz yurts (boz üy) for hotels & resorts — anchor on `glamping yurt` + `kyrgyz yurt` + `luxury yurt`
- **Product (trade):** `glamping yurt`, `yurt tent`, `luxury yurt`, `glamping tent`
- **Configure:** `glamping yurt`, `glamping pods` (comparison), `glamping accommodation`
- **Heritage:** `kyrgyz yurt`, `mongolian yurt`, `traditional yurt`, `authentic yurt`, `wooden yurt` (+ "ger vs boz üy")
- **FAQ (GEO):** `what is a yurt`, `how much does a yurt cost`, `how long does a yurt last`, `yurt vs tent`, informational long-tail → FAQPage schema
- **Glamping angle (plumbing only, not hero voice):** `glamping yurt`, `glamping accommodation`, `luxury glamping` in meta/alt/one FAQ line

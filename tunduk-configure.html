Breathable Rain Cover

Additional wool felt layer between the chiy reed mats and outer felt. Recommended for cold climates (below 5°C winters).

Reinforced chimney aperture through the felt roof with heat-resistant lining and sealing ring. Compatible with most standard wood-burning stoves.

Shyrdak & Ala Kiyiz Floor Set

Full floor coverage combining shyrdak (mosaic felt appliqué carpets) and ala kiyiz (rolled felt rugs) in complementary traditional motifs. Colour palette customisable. Made by the women of the Kyzyl-Tuu artisan family.

Upgrade the standard chiy reed insulation mats to oimo chiy — hand-decorated with coloured wool woven into geometric patterns. Lines the interior kerege walls with an additional layer of beauty.

Set of traditional Kyrgyz floor cushions/mattresses in national fabric with ornamental covers. The authentic alternative to chairs — how nomadic families have sat in the yurt for centuries.

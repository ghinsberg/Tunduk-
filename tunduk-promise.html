The Tündük Promise

You are buying from people, not a checkout.

A Boz Üy is made by hand, to order, half a world away. That asks for trust, so here is exactly how an order works, and how your money is looked after at every step.

1

Pay by card, and your bank stands behind you

Your deposit is taken by card through Stripe, the same payment system used by some of the largest companies in the world. Paying by card means your own bank protects the payment. We never see your card details.

2

Half to start, and the work begins at once

You pay a 50% deposit to confirm the order. Adylet begins your Boz Üy the week it is confirmed. The deposit buys the materials and books the maker's hands; it is not a fee for nothing.

3

You watch it being made

You do not wait in the dark. You get photographs when the frame is done, and a full video of your finished Boz Üy before anything ships, every component shown, every measurement confirmed.

4

You pay the balance only after you approve

The remaining 50% is due only once you have seen the video and approved your yurt. If it is not what we agreed, it does not ship. The balance is sent as a separate Stripe invoice at that point, never charged without your say-so.

5

A delivery window, in writing

Your order states a delivery window: [X] weeks from deposit to dispatch. If we miss it by more than 60 days, you can ask for your full deposit back.

6

Prefer escrow? Say the word

If you would rather hold the funds in escrow and release them against milestones, ask us and we will set that up. We would rather you felt safe than rushed.

Shipping, packing, fumigation and export are quoted separately with your balance. Import duties and any VAT in your country are paid by you on arrival. The full detail is in our terms of sale.

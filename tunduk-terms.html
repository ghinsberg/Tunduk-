Terms of Sale

Terms of sale

Last updated: 2 November 2026

These terms cover the purchase of a Boz Üy from Tündük. The seller of record is Tunduk.trade LLC. They are written plainly on purpose. If anything here is unclear, ask us before you order and we will explain it.

How an order forms

We do not sell from a cart. You send your project through the configurator or by email, we talk it through on a video call, and we give you a written quote for your exact specification. A quote is valid for 14 days. An order forms when you accept the quote and pay the deposit.

Payment

  • A 50% deposit is due to confirm the order and begin the build. It is taken by card through Stripe.
  • The 50% balance is due only after you have seen and approved the video of your finished Boz Üy, before it ships. It is sent as a separate Stripe invoice.
  • Prices are quoted in US dollars.

Cancellation and refunds

  • Within 48 hours of ordering: cancel for any reason and get a full refund.
  • Later, before the frame is built: your deposit is refunded minus the materials payment already made to the maker on your behalf.
  • After the frame is built: the deposit is non-refundable, because your Boz Üy is already being made by hand for you.

Delivery window

Your order states a delivery window of [X] weeks from deposit to dispatch. If we miss that window by more than 60 days, you may cancel and have your full deposit back. See The Tündük Promise.

Shipping, export and import

Shipping, packing, fumigation and export costs are quoted and billed separately with your balance, not included in the yurt price. Import duties and any VAT in your country are paid by you on arrival. We provide the full export documentation and will point you to the right codes so you can estimate your import costs before the shipment lands.

What you receive

A Boz Üy is handmade from natural materials, so small variations in the wood, felt and dye are part of the craft, not faults. Before anything ships you receive a full set of photographs and a video walkthrough, and the order ships only once you have approved it.

Questions

Write to trade@tunduk.trade and a person will answer.
